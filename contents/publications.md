### Preprints

**Jizhong Li.**<br>
**[LowAux-RDNet: Low-Pass Residual Supervision with Scene-Balanced Real-World Training for Single-Image Reflection Removal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22707).**<br>
*arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.22707 [cs.CV], 2026.*

- Introduces a training-only low-pass residual auxiliary objective and scene-balanced real-world training for RDNet-based single-image reflection removal.
- Builds a unified public benchmark across five datasets and reports a macro average of **27.546 dB PSNR**, **0.9220 SSIM**, **0.9751 NCC**, and **0.004760 LMSE**.
- [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22707) · [Code](https://github.com/flyingc2004/lowaux-rdnet)

### Manuscripts

**EvalAwarePosterGen: Reader-Centered Agent Framework for Paper-to-Poster Generation**<br>
*Submitted to AAAI 2027.*

- Proposes a closed-loop, evaluation-aware agent framework centered on what readers should understand from a scientific poster.
- Introduces reader-goal planning, goal-conditioned storyboarding, reader simulation, deterministic validation, self-critique, and repair.
- Develops intrinsic planner evaluation, reader-goal coverage metrics, and with/without-planner ablations as foundations for a broader benchmark.
- [Project repository](https://github.com/flyingc2004/Eval_Aware_Poster_Gen)
