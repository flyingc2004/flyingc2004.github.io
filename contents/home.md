I am an undergraduate student in **Computer Science and Technology at Fudan University**. My research focuses on embodied intelligence and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, with a particular interest in combining vision, language, tactile perception, and structured memory for reliable long-horizon robotic manipulation.

I aim to build embodied agents that can not only perceive and follow instructions, but also track task progress, learn from interaction history, and recover from physical failures in real-world environments.

#### Research Interests

- Embodied Intelligence and Vision-Language-Action Models
- Tactile-Enhanced VLA and Multimodal Robot Learning
- Long-Horizon Robotic Manipulation and Failure Recovery
- Structured Memory for Embodied Agents
- Imitation Learning and Reinforcement Learning
